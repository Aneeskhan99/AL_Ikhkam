# Competitor research

Researched 6 October 2026. These are the sites a bank's compliance head or a finance professional is likely to compare Al-Ihkam against.

## Who we looked at

| Firm | Where | What stands out | What is weak |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Al Hilal Shariah Advisors](https://alhilalsa.com) | Karachi | First SECP-registered Shariah advisory company (2018). In March 2026 SECP approved it as the first independent Shariah screener of securities. Shows about 50 client logos and a licence PDF. | "John Doe CEO" placeholder testimonials still live. ## What we took from it

1. **No Pakistani competitor offers Urdu.** A properly typeset right-to-left Urdu site in Nastaliq is a clear first. The new site has a complete Urdu version of every page.
2. **Everyone hides course details.** The new site gives each programme its own page with level, duration, format, audience, modules and certificate.
3. **Faculty credentials are the strongest honest trust signal.** The faculty includes a PhD who is an Assistant Professor at Bahria University, a former Bank Alfalah Shariah Compliance Officer who now trains at EIBFS Dubai, and a CUST lecturer who holds the NIBAF FIBO. These are shown as named institutions on the home page and as full profiles on the faculty page. No invented statistics or client logos were added.
4. **Name the standards, but don't claim a licence.** The site says reviews reference the AAOIFI Shariah Standards, the SBP Shariah Governance Framework and the SECP Shariah Governance Regulations 2023. It claims no registration that has not been confirmed.
5. **Publish the method.** Like Musaffa and Zoya, the advisory page sets out the four-stage review process and what the client receives.
6. **Tools bring people back.** The Zakat calculator was rebuilt for Pakistan: silver or gold Nisab, editable PKR rates per tola, a step-by-step breakdown, a print view, and the official bank deduction Nisab shown separately.
7. **No placeholders, nothing stale.** A small, honest, well-kept site beats a cluttered one.
